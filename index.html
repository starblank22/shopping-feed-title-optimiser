    Why titles are the highest-leverage lever you have

    In Google Shopping and Performance Max you don't write ad copy — Google writes it for you, straight from your feed. The product title is the single biggest input into which searches you show for and whether anyone clicks. Fix the titles and you fix the account.

    • Front-load within 70 characters. Shoppers and Google's matching both weight the start of the title hardest. Brand and core product first — everything else after.
    • Use the full 150 characters. A 38-character title is unscored real estate. Material, colour, size and model all add query coverage.
    • Cut the promotional noise. "Sale", "best price", "free delivery" and ALL-CAPS shouting waste characters and can get titles disapproved. Titles should be factual.
    • Match the landing page. Title, price and specs must match the destination exactly — mismatches kill Quality Score and trigger suspensions.
